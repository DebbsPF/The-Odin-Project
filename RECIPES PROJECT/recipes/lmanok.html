Lechon Manok

Description

Lechon manok is a Filipino-style roasted chicken marinated in a flavorful mixture of garlic, onions, lemongrass, soy sauce, citrus, and spices. It is traditionally cooked over charcoal until the skin is golden and crisp while the meat remains tender and juicy. Often served with rice and a dipping sauce made from soy sauce, vinegar, or chicken drippings, lechon manok is a popular dish for family meals, celebrations, and gatherings.

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken
  • Garlic
  • Onion
  • Lemongrass
  • Soy sauce
  • Citrus juice
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Clean the chicken and pat it dry.
  2. Combine the garlic, onion, lemongrass, soy sauce, citrus juice, salt, and pepper.
  3. Rub the marinade over the chicken and let it marinate for at least 2 hours.
  4. Stuff the chicken with lemongrass and onion, then secure the cavity.
  5. Roast over charcoal, turning occasionally, until the skin is golden and the chicken is cooked through.
  6. Rest the chicken before carving and serve with rice and dipping sauce.
