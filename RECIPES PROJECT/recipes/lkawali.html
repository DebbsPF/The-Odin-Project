Lechon Kawali

Description

Lechon kawali is a Filipino dish made with crispy deep-fried pork belly. It is traditionally served with a savory dipping sauce.

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds pork belly
  • 4 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • Water, for boiling
  • Oil, for frying

Steps

  1. Place the pork belly, garlic, bay leaf, salt, and enough water to cover it in a pot.
  2. Bring to a boil, then simmer for 45–60 minutes until tender.
  3. Remove the pork and let it cool and dry completely.
  4. Deep-fry the pork belly until golden brown and crispy.
  5. Slice and serve with your favorite dipping sauce.
